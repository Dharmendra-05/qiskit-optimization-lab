\subsection{Key Empirical Discoveries}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Constructive Interference with Depth:} Increasing QAOA depth from $p=1$ to $p=2$ increases the Feasibility Yield from $41.65\%$ to $77.00\%$, demonstrating that alternating unitaries effectively concentrate quantum probability mass onto the valid budget subspace $\binom{5}{2}$.
    \item \textbf{Classical Greedy Failure:} The greedy heuristic fails to reach the global optimum ($\alpha = 0.9649$) because it ignores pairwise covariance interactions between assets. In contrast, QAOA accounts for full two-qubit covariance entanglement and recovers the exact global optimum ($\alpha = 1.0000$).
    \item \textbf{Penalty Multiplier Dynamics:} Sweeping $\lambda \in [0.5, 5.0]$ reveals that setting $\lambda < 1.0$ fails to sufficiently suppress infeasible states (feasibility yield $< 35\%$), whereas excessive penalties ($\lambda = 5.0$) distort the energy landscape, trapping the optimizer in suboptimal local basins ($\alpha = 0.9649$). The tested value $\lambda = 2.5$ provides the most balanced trade-off.
\end{itemize}
