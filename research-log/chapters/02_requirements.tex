\section{Official Requirements and Traceability}

\subsection{Evaluation Criteria}
Per the official Hackathon Guide (\texttt{HACKATHON\_GUIDE.md}), projects are evaluated on four key pillars:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Quantum Rigor \& Authenticity:} Genuine leverage of quantum superposition, entanglement, and interference via Qiskit circuits rather than classical random numbers.
    \item \textbf{Working Implementation \& Live Demo:} Fully functioning, reproducible prototype with demonstrable live execution in under 10 minutes.
    \item \textbf{Problem Formulation \& Originality:} Going beyond textbook Max-Cut to solve a constrained, real-world dilemma.
    \item \textbf{Presentation Clarity \& Critical Understanding:} Candid scientific reflection on physical quantum noise, hardware constraints, and NISQ limits.
\end{enumerate}
